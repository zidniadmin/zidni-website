FAQ

Questions We Often Hear

What does the free consultation involve?

A complimentary discovery session in which we review your business, current systems and objectives. You receive practical, prioritised recommendations, with no obligation to proceed.

How do I choose the right ERP for my business?

We begin with your processes, budget and growth plans, then assess suitable platforms, such as Zoho, Sage and Odoo, against those criteria. You receive a clear, impartial recommendation, together with the reasoning behind it.

Can you work with the system we already use?

Yes. We can improve, integrate or extend your current systems, and we will advise you candidly if migration would better serve your business.

How long does a project take, and how is it priced?

Timelines and fees depend on scope. We agree the plan, timeline and pricing with you before work begins. Engagement options include project-based delivery, a support retainer and advisory services.

How do you use AI in your work?

We use AI to accelerate design, configuration, testing, documentation and support, and we build AI agents that handle routine tasks. All output is reviewed by our consultants, and we agree with you in advance which data AI tools may access.

Will my team receive training?

Yes. Hands-on training is included with every project, so your team can operate the system with confidence from go-live.

Can you help us prepare for UAE e-invoicing?

Yes. We review your invoice and tax data, connect your accounting or ERP system to an accredited provider, and test the end-to-end invoicing workflow. We confirm current UAE Federal Tax Authority requirements with you throughout the engagement.

Do you support businesses outside the UAE?

Yes. We support businesses in the UAE and internationally, with on-site visits in the UAE where a project requires them.

Do you provide support after go-live?

Yes. A support retainer provides ongoing assistance, while an advisory engagement offers expert input when you face an important decision.

Process Improvement and Automation

Example use cases

Drawn from our hands-on experience across ERP and accounting projects. Here is what we regularly find in businesses, and how we resolve it.

1

Sales and payment systems not aligned with accounting

What we often seeDay-to-day sales run in one system and accounting in another, so payments and the ERP rarely agree.

What we doWe first understand what each system is used for, then check whether every daily transaction reaches the ERP. We agree a timely daily close and use APIs to automate the posting, based on what your accounting software supports.

What changesDaily transactions sync automatically, and balances and fund positions stay aligned.

2

Inventory that does not match the finance books

What we often seeStock is managed loosely, so inventory records drift away from purchasing and finance.

What we doWe map the types of inventory purchases and payments, confirm each is processed correctly, and align stock records with the finance books.

What changesInventory values in the books reflect what is really on hand and what was paid for.

3

Transactions that sit outside the books

What we often seeRecurring or monthly arrangements, such as freelancer or service payments, are agreed with clients or vendors but never recorded.

What we doWe identify these arrangements and configure the system, with clear guidelines, so they are recorded on time and consistently.

What changesNothing is left on the client side or in a spreadsheet, and reporting is complete.

4

Control over backdated transactions

What we often seeManual backdated entries change closed periods and distort reports, especially physical stock quantities and valuation.

What we doWe set period locks, approvals and posting rules, and replace manual adjustments with system-driven entries.

What changesReports stay reliable once a period is closed, and changes are traceable.

5

Fixed asset depreciation

What we often seeDepreciation is calculated by hand each period, which is slow and easy to get wrong.

What we doWe configure asset categories and depreciation rules so the system calculates and posts depreciation automatically.

What changesDepreciation is posted on schedule with no manual calculation.

Are you ready for UAE e-invoicing?

Answer five quick questions to see where you stand, then see how Zoho Books helps you close any gaps.

An Accredited Service Provider (ASP) is a company approved by the UAE Ministry of Finance to issue, send and receive your e-invoices and report the data to the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). Every in-scope business must appoint one, so it is the key step to being compliant. Zoho is itself an ASP, so your invoices can flow directly from Zoho Books.
